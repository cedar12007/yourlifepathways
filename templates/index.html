About Me

For over 25 years, my career has bridged technology, organizational leadership, and executive strategy. My background spans fast-growth startups and tech consulting to leadership within major multinational software enterprises. From collaborating with C-suite leaders to guiding global teams, I know firsthand what it takes to scale an organization, navigate corporate dynamics, and perform under high-stakes pressure.

As the head of a global platform engineering organization at SAP, I lead teams building highly-secure cloud platforms for the world's most regulated industries. Beyond technology, I am deeply committed to developing high-impact talent and leadership. In parallel, I serve as an SAP Global Coach, helping leaders, managers, and growth-minded professionals navigate organizational complexity and scale their impact.

As a father of three, a long-time youth sports coach, and a Big Brothers Big Sisters volunteer, I’ve always been drawn to helping people grow. Whether coaching on the court or partnering with leaders in an office, the underlying mission is the same: helping you recognize your true potential, build resilience, and develop the tools to reach it.
