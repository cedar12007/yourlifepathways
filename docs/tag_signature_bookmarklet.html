Universal Gmail Signature Tagging Bookmarklet

Easily tag any drafted email in Gmail with a custom client name, lead ID, or campaign tag. Works automatically with both YourLifePathways and PrepForInterviews signatures!

Drag this universal button to your Bookmarks Bar:

🏷️ Tag Signature
Key Features:
  • One Single Bookmarklet: Automatically detects whether the draft contains a YourLifePathways or PrepForInterviews signature and tracks to the right system.
  • TrustedHTML Compliant: Uses direct DOM attribute updates that bypass Google's Content Security Policy restrictions without error.
  • Removes data-smartmail: Prevents Gmail from overwriting your custom tagged signature with your default settings when sending.
  • Triggers Autosave: Dispatches native input events so Gmail immediately syncs the draft to Google servers.

How to use:

  1. In Gmail, open or start a draft (with your signature inserted).
  2. Click 🏷️ Tag Signature in your Bookmarks Bar.
  3. The prompt will identify the detected site ([YourLifePathways] or [PrepForInterviews]). Type your client name or tag (e.g., cedar or john_smith) and hit Enter.
  4. Review the confirmation dialog showing your updated URL, then check the bottom right corner of the compose box to ensure it says "Saved".
  5. Send your email!
