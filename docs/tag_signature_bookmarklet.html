Gmail Signature Tagging Bookmarklet

Easily tag any drafted email in Gmail with a custom client name, lead ID, or campaign tag so every email open and website click is tracked in your admin dashboard.

Drag this button directly to your browser's Bookmarks Bar:

🏷️ Tag Signature

How to use:

  1. In Gmail, open or start drafting an email (make sure your signature is inserted).
  2. Click the 🏷️ Tag Signature button in your Bookmarks Bar.
  3. A popup asks for the recipient/campaign tag (e.g., john_doe or interview_prep).
  4. Hit Enter. Both the tracking headshot image and website links in your draft are instantly updated with your custom tag!
Or copy the code manually

If your Bookmarks Bar is hidden (press Cmd + Shift + B to show it), or if you prefer adding it manually, copy this entire snippet into the URL field of a new bookmark:

javascript:(function(){const activeDraft=document.querySelector('div[aria-label="Message Body"]:focus, div[role="textbox"][contenteditable="true"]:focus')||document.querySelector('div[aria-label="Message Body"], div[role="textbox"][contenteditable="true"]');if(!activeDraft){alert('No active Gmail draft found. Click inside your email body first!');return;}const img=activeDraft.querySelector('img[src*="profile_pic_email_signature"], img[src*="/signature/"], img[src*="yourlifepathways.com"]');if(!img){alert('Signature headshot image not found in the current draft.');return;}let currentTag='';try{const u=new URL(img.src);currentTag=u.searchParams.get('ref')||u.searchParams.get('tag')||'';}catch(e){}const tag=prompt('Enter tracking tag / client name (e.g. john_smith, interview_prep):',currentTag);if(tag===null)return;const clean=tag.trim().toLowerCase().replace(/[^a-z0-9_-]/g,'_');try{const imgUrl=new URL(img.src);if(clean){imgUrl.searchParams.set('ref',clean);}else{imgUrl.searchParams.delete('ref');}img.src=imgUrl.toString();const links=activeDraft.querySelectorAll('a[href*="yourlifepathways.com"]');links.forEach(l=>{try{const lu=new URL(l.href);if(clean){lu.searchParams.set('ref',clean);}else{lu.searchParams.set('ref','sig');}l.href=lu.toString();}catch(err){}});alert('Signature updated!\nTracking Tag: '+(clean||'default'));}catch(e){alert('Error updating signature: '+e.message);}})();
