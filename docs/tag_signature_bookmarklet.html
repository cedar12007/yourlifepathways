Gmail Signature Tagging Bookmarklet (v2)

Easily tag any drafted email in Gmail with a custom client name, lead ID, or campaign tag so every email open and website click is tracked in your admin dashboard.

Drag this updated button to replace your Bookmarks Bar shortcut:

🏷️ Tag Signature
What's fixed in v2:
  • Uses native browser execCommand to register with Gmail's internal editor state so changes stick when sent.
  • Detaches Gmail's data-smartmail="gmail_signature" attribute so Gmail doesn't overwrite your custom tag with the default settings.
  • Dispatches native input events to force Gmail's autosave to commit before sending.

How to use:

  1. In Gmail, start or open your draft email (with your signature present).
  2. Click the 🏷️ Tag Signature button in your Bookmarks Bar.
  3. Type the tag (e.g., john_doe) and hit Enter.
  4. Confirm Gmail shows "Saved" at the bottom right of the compose box, then click Send.
