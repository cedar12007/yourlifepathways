4. Confidentiality, Ethics & ICF Auditing

The Coach agrees to maintain strict confidentiality of all coaching conversations in full alignment with the International Coaching Federation (ICF) Code of Ethics. No personal, proprietary, or organizational information shared during sessions will be disclosed without the Client’s written consent.

ICF Client Log Audit Consent: The Client agrees that their name, email address, and total coaching hours completed under this contract may be listed on the Coach's confidential client coaching log for credentialing audit purposes with the ICF. The ICF audits contact records solely to verify hours and does not contact the Client regarding session content.

Optional Session Recording Consent (ICF Credentialing Performance Evaluation)
For the Coach’s ongoing credentialing with the International Coaching Federation (ICF), the ICF requires submission of unedited audio/video recordings to assess the Coach’s core competency performance.
  • Recordings are used exclusively for confidential evaluation by certified ICF performance assessors and/or mentor coaches.
  • Recordings will never be published, distributed, or shared publicly.
  • The Coach will give verbal notice before starting any recording, and the Client retains the right at any point to decline recording for a specific session or request that recording stop immediately.

5. Cancellation & Scheduling Policy

Both parties commit to honoring scheduled appointments to protect the rhythm of the engagement. Sessions must be rescheduled with at least 24 hours' advance notice. Missed appointments or cancellations with less than 24 hours' notice are deducted from the program total, except in acute personal or organizational emergencies.
